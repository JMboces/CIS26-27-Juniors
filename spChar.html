HTML Special Characters

entity: a mechanism for writing special characters or symbols in html, such as mathematical symbols, characters in most languages, and many other many html entities can be specified by name by a decimal number or by a hexadecimal number

example: a copyright logo.
© - &copy;
© - &#169;
© - &#xA9;

emojis

emoji characters can be displayed in a webpage with a decimal hexadecimal entity,example: a smiley face.
🙂 - &#128578;
🙃 - &#128579;

entities for reserved characters

< and & characters can be displayed using entities. &lt; for Less than, and &amp; for the ampersand. EX. 2 < 3 = True
