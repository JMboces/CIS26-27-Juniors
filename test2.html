Network and Computer Systems Administrators

Network and computer systems administrators manage and maintain an organization's computer networks and systems.

What they do

Network and computer systems administrators manage and maintain an organization's computer networks and systems. They ensure that these systems are functioning properly and securely.

  • Monitor network performance and troubleshoot issues
  • Configure and maintain network hardware and software
  • Implement security measures to protect the network
  • Update and maintain system documentation
  • Collaborate with other IT professionals to resolve issues

Skills you need

Technical skills

  • Networking fundamentals
  • Operating systems administration
  • Security protocols and best practices
  • System monitoring and troubleshooting

People skills

  • Communication and teamwork
  • Patience and problem-solving
  • Willingness to learn from feedback

How to get started

  1. Practice by building small networks or maintaining existing systems.
  2. Learn networking fundamentals through a class or beginner course.
  3. Learn system administration concepts and tools.
  4. Study security protocols and best practices.
  5. Apply for entry-level roles and keep building your skills.

Certifications and credentials

  • CompTIA Server+ — offered by CompTIA.
  • Red Hat Certified System Administrator (RHCSA) — offered by Red Hat.

Certification requirements can change, so check each organization’s current exam details.

Pay and job outlook

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reports that network and computer systems administrators had a median annual wage of $86,550 in May 2024. The BLS also projects strong growth for network and computer systems administrator employment. Pay and opportunities vary by location, experience, and industry. See the BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook for details.

Where you could work

Network and computer systems administrators work in industries such as technology, finance, healthcare, and education. Employers range from technology companies and banks to hospitals, schools, and government agencies.
